## After-launch responses

If anyone replies "what's the catch?", "is this gambling?", "do I need an account?":
- "No catch — free, no account, no money. Pick a roster, see real-game stats. That's it."

If anyone says "this is just a slot machine":
- "Honest reaction. The reason I avoided that framing is the stats are real — your roster did what they did, you didn't pull a slot lever. But I get the comparison."

If anyone says "what's the point if I always win?":
- "You don't. The leaderboard is everyone's hands today, so 'winning' is being above the bar that day. Plus there are tiers — you can win the hand but miss the higher tier."

If anyone says "boring":
- "Probably right. The bar I'm trying to clear is 'fun for 5 minutes between meetings,' not 'fun for an hour.' Different game."
